# Attribution and Third-Party Assets

This project may bundle or download third-party assets used for development and app content.

## Fonts

- Amiri: sourced via repository URL in scripts/download-fonts.js
- Cairo: sourced via repository URL in scripts/download-fonts.js

Check the upstream repositories for current license files and attribution requirements.

## Images

- Twemoji PNG assets: downloaded via scripts/download-images.js

Twemoji assets are generally distributed under CC-BY 4.0 and may require attribution in your app/store listing. Confirm the latest license terms from the official source.

## Audio

- scripts/download-arabic-tts.js and scripts/fix-audio-format.js use Google Translate TTS endpoints to generate Arabic pronunciation audio for development.

Before distributing generated audio publicly, verify compliance with Google terms and any jurisdiction-specific constraints.

## Maintainer Responsibility

Before making this repository or app distribution public, review and confirm that:

1. Each third-party asset source permits your intended use.
2. Required attributions are included in app content or release metadata.
3. No paid/proprietary assets are redistributed without permission.
